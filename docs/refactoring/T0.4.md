## Owned files
- `configure.ac`

## Goal
Raise the standard from C++14 to **C++20** to unblock the modernization in later tickets, and clean up a dead configure conditional.

## Toolchain context
- Official Docker build: Arch Linux (very recent GCC/Clang).
- This repo's `Dockerfile`: `debian:12-slim` (GCC 12 → full C++20).
- C++20 is therefore safe on both targets.

## To do
- Replace `AX_CXX_COMPILE_STDCXX_14([noext],[mandatory])` (`configure.ac:45`) with `AX_CXX_COMPILE_STDCXX_20([noext],[mandatory])`.
- **Dead conditional** (minor/test) — `AM_CONDITIONAL([HAVE_AUTOBAHN], ...)` (`configure.ac:150`) is referenced by no `Makefile.am`; `tests/websockets/{run_testsuite_*.sh,fuzzingclient.json}` are in no `TESTS` and no `EXTRA_DIST` (so `make check` never runs them and `make dist` drops them). Either wire the autobahn suite into a `HAVE_AUTOBAHN`-guarded `check-local` **and** add the files to `EXTRA_DIST`, or remove the dead conditional. Decide and document.
- Full validation build.

## Unblocks

## Acceptance

- The autobahn conditional is either wired + distributed, or removed.
